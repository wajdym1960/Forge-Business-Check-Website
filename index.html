What Forge checks today

Version 0.1 is intentionally narrow. It focuses on evidence we can verify and explain, rather than pretending to offer comprehensive business due diligence.

SEC company evidence

Forge checks the SEC company index for exact normalized company-name evidence. SEC absence is treated as non-adverse rather than automatically suspicious.

OFAC sanctions evidence

Forge screens official OFAC SDN records. A name match triggers review; a name match alone is never treated as a confirmed sanctions match.

Questions before you request a check

Clear scope for an early-stage business decision.

What is reviewed?

Selected authoritative public evidence, including SEC company records and OFAC sanctions data.

Is a person involved?

Yes. Every early-access report requires human review before it can be delivered.

What does it replace?

Nothing. It is an early-stage screen, not legal, credit, compliance, or comprehensive KYB advice.

Is there a charge?

No payment is collected during the current validation stage.

Current scope and limitations. Forge Business Check currently uses selected authoritative public evidence, including SEC company records and OFAC sanctions data, together with deterministic evidence-sufficiency and scoring rules. It is not a substitute for legal advice, credit analysis, regulatory compliance review, or comprehensive KYB due diligence.
