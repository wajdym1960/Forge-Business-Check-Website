Proposed service

A bounded consistency check before release

$49one-time, for one eligible artifact set

Included mechanical checks

  • Component names and counts across the rulebook and manifest.
  • Setup quantities and repeated numeric values.
  • Cross-document references that appear missing, conflicting, or inconsistent.
  • An evidence-linked exception report with page or source citations and human review before delivery.

The proposed fixed scope is one eligible 6–40 page rulebook PDF plus one CSV component manifest. If the service opens, the delivery target will be two business days after payment and written acceptance of eligible files.

Not included

  • Copyediting, rewriting, style review, playtesting, game balancing, or design advice.
  • Legal, intellectual-property, regulatory, or compliance review.
  • Manufacturing approval, certification, publication, or any guarantee of campaign or sales results.
  • Unsupported formats, unsafe or unauthorized files, custom consulting, or revisions to game content.

Any findings are potential inconsistencies for publisher review. The customer remains responsible for rights, corrections, testing, production, and release decisions.

Service terms

Terms applicable if the service is later opened

Eligibility and customer authority

A customer must represent that it owns or is authorized to submit every file and to instruct Forge AI Ventures to process it for this review. Personal data, malicious content, material the customer is not authorized to share, and unrelated third-party confidential information must not be submitted. Forge may reject unsupported, unsafe, over-scope, unauthorized, or otherwise ineligible material without processing it.

Service and reliance limits

Forge AI Ventures would review only the supplied rulebook and manifest for the mechanical checks stated above. Findings would identify potential inconsistencies; they would not certify accuracy or completeness and would not constitute legal, intellectual-property, manufacturing, editorial, or professional advice. No fitness, sales, campaign, production, or release outcome is promised.

Fixed scope and delivery

One order would cover one eligible artifact set for a one-time price of $49. The two-business-day delivery clock would begin only after payment and written acceptance of eligible files. Custom scope, consulting calls, editing, and continuing service would not be included.

Refunds and disputes

Forge AI Ventures would provide a full $49 refund if a submission is ineligible or if Forge cannot deliver the defined report within two business days after written acceptance. Forge may also refund and stop work if a security, privacy, legal, payment, or experiment-validity issue arises. The payment processor’s posting time is outside Forge’s control. If the service opens, refund or dispute requests must be sent to the verified support contact shown in Stripe Checkout and on the payment receipt.
